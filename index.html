Meet your teacher

Charlie Burns

Yoga teacher, movement educator, and artist.

For over 12 years, Charlie has helped people discover what movement can do for them. From 2018 to 2023, Charlie founded and ran BurnStrong, an independent yoga and wellness business in Midtown Manhattan.

Charlie believes yoga is an art form—a chance to explore, express yourself, and build a deeper connection with your body. Every person is different, and every practice should reflect that. The goal is to help you feel seen, supported, and challenged, whether you're building strength, improving mobility, or simply making space for yourself.

Charlie has worked with children, athletes, dancers, prenatal clients, and people with a wide range of physical abilities. Each session is shaped around your needs, experience, and goals, with an emphasis on mindful movement, creativity, and care.

Now based in Puerto Rico, Charlie welcomes you to practice in a way that feels personal, meaningful, and uniquely yours.
